Privacy

Last updated 21 August 2026

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or address. There are no accounts, so there is nothing about you for anyone to steal from us. Your location is used on your phone and is never sent to us.

What we hold

  • A device token. Your phone generates a random token the first time you use the app. That is your identity here. It is not linked to you, your email or your phone.
  • A display name — whatever you type. A nickname is fine; nothing checks it.
  • What you contribute — reviews, ratings, photos, confirmations, corrections and reports, with the time you made them and which place they refer to.
  • Feedback you send us, plus which screen you were on and whether you were online.

What we do not hold

Your location. The map uses your device's location to show what is near you and sort it by distance. That happens entirely on your phone. It is never transmitted to us, and we could not look up where you have been even if we wanted to.

Photo metadata. Every photo is re-encoded on your device before upload, which strips EXIF data — including the GPS coordinates and camera identifiers phones normally embed. We receive the picture and nothing else.

Contact details. We could not email you if we tried.

Payment details. No field in this app accepts a card number.

Photos

Photos are checked on your device for explicit content and for people in shot, then held on our server visible only to you and a moderator until a person approves them. Approved photos are public to everyone using the app.

Please photograph the room, not its occupants. A photo containing a person is held automatically and will be rejected. Anyone can report a photo; a reported photo is taken down immediately and re-reviewed.

Who can see what

Everything you contribute except feedback is public, shown next to your display name. Treat a review like a public post. Do not put your phone number, email or home address in one — the app tries to stop you, but it is not perfect.

Advertising

On the website the app shows sponsored listings from nearby businesses, always labelled as sponsored. They are chosen by the part of the map you are looking at — not by an advertising identifier and not by any profile of you.

In the iPhone and Android apps we also use Google AdMob to show a banner at the bottom of the screen. AdMob is a third-party advertising network: it receives your device's advertising identifier, IP address and coarse region in order to serve and measure ads. Google's use of that data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

You are asked before any of this is personalised. On iPhone you will see Apple's tracking prompt; saying no is a real choice — you still get ads, they are just chosen without a profile of you. Either way, your location is never sent to us or to an advertiser. Subscribing to Plus removes ads entirely.

How long we keep it

For as long as the service runs. This is an early release; the database may be reset, and there is no guarantee your contributions survive.

Children

Not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Third parties

  • OpenStreetMap (map data) and CARTO (map images) receive requests from your device, including which map area you are viewing.
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) receives what you type into search.
  • jsDelivr serves the on-device safety-check models.

We do not sell data.

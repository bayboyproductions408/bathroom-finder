# Privacy policy — Bathroom Finder

**Draft.** Written to be accurate about what the software actually does, so it is a
starting point for a lawyer rather than a finished policy. Do not publish it as-is
without review.

_Last updated: 21 August 2026_

## The short version

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or address. We do not have accounts,
so there is nothing to hack out of us. Your location is used on your device to show
what is near you and is never sent to us.

## What we hold

**A device token.** When you first use the app your device generates a random token.
That is your identity here. It is not linked to you, your email or your phone.

**A display name.** Whatever you type. Use a nickname if you prefer — nothing checks it.

**What you contribute.** Reviews, ratings, photos, confirmations, corrections and
reports, with the time you made them and the place they refer to.

**Feedback you send us,** plus which screen you were on and whether you were online.

## What we do not hold

- **Your location.** The map uses your device's location to draw where you are and sort
  what is near you. That happens entirely on your device. It is never transmitted to us.
- **Any contact details.** We could not email you if we wanted to.
- **Photo metadata.** Every photo is re-encoded on your device before it is uploaded,
  which strips EXIF data — including the GPS coordinates and camera identifiers phones
  normally embed. We receive the picture and nothing else.
- **Payment details.** There is no field in this app that accepts a card number.
  Payments are simulated and no money moves.

## Photos

Photos are checked on your device for explicit content and for people in shot, then
held on our server where they are **visible only to you and to a moderator** until a
person approves them. Approved photos are public to everyone using the app.

Please do not photograph people. If a photo contains someone, it is held automatically,
and it will be rejected.

Anyone can report a photo. A reported photo is taken down immediately and re-reviewed.

## Who can see what

Everything you contribute except feedback is **public** to other users, shown next to
your display name. Treat a review like a public post. Do not put your phone number,
email or home address in one — the app tries to stop you, but it is not perfect.

## How long we keep it

For as long as the service runs. This is a test; the database may be deleted entirely
at any time, and there is no guarantee your contributions survive.

## Deleting your data

In the app, **Profile → Erase everything on this device** removes your local copy. To
remove what is on the server, contact us with your display name — because there is no
account system, we may need you to identify your contributions.

## Children

Not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Third parties

- **OpenStreetMap** (map data) and **CARTO** (map images) receive requests from your
  device, including the map area you are looking at. Their privacy policies apply.
- **Nominatim** (OpenStreetMap) receives what you type into search.
- **jsDelivr** serves the safety-check models to your device.

We do not sell data, and there is no advertising or third-party analytics.

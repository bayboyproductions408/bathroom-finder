# Terms of use — Bathroom Finder

**Draft, and this is test software.** Not legal advice; have it reviewed before relying
on it.

_Last updated: 21 August 2026_

## What this is

A community-maintained map of bathrooms. Everything in it — whether a bathroom exists,
whether it is open, whether it is clean — comes from members of the public, including
you. **We do not verify any of it.**

## Use it with judgement

Information here may be wrong, out of date, or a joke. A bathroom listed as open may be
locked, private, or gone. Do not rely on it in an emergency, and do not enter private
property because an app told you there was a bathroom.

Access rights are yours to establish. A listing is not permission to enter anywhere.

## What you contribute

You keep ownership of your photos and words, and you grant us a licence to display them
in the app. By contributing you confirm:

- It is your own photo, or you have the right to post it.
- **It contains no people.** Photograph the room, not its occupants.
- It is not explicit, offensive, or an attempt to harass or identify anybody.
- It is honest. Do not review a place to damage a business you dislike.

We may remove anything, at any time, for any reason.

## Renting a bathroom

The hosting and payment features are **a prototype**. No money moves and payments are
simulated. There is no identity verification, no background check, no insurance and no
support line.

**Do not use it to invite a stranger into your home, and do not enter a stranger's home
through it.** If you ignore that, you do so entirely at your own risk.
